Find a solver

Every VNN-LIB 2.0 solver reports what it supports through its supports command. Those answers are collected automatically, and this page searches them in reverse: describe the query you need to solve, and it returns the solvers that can take it.

Leave a filter blank to ignore it. Theory filters use the downward closure of what each solver reported, so asking for BND also matches a solver that reported only POLY.

Theories supported

Chapter 4 of the standard. Each is matched against the downward closure of what the solver reported, so a weaker theory also matches a solver that reported a stronger one.
